# Fleet and Sustainability

## Fleet at the end of 2024

| Depot | Diesel vans | Electric vans | Cargo bikes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Larrow Central | 28 | 10 | 0 |
| Eastmere | 24 | 0 | 0 |
| Holt Cross | 0 | 19 | 0 |
| Saltings | 0 | 12 | 6 |
| **Total** | **52** | **41** | **6** |

41 of 93 vans (44%) were electric at the end of 2024.

## Target

Brackenfield's target is a 100% electric van fleet by the end of 2028. The plan is:

- 2025: replace 10 diesel vans at Larrow Central.
- 2026: replace the remaining 18 diesel vans at Larrow Central.
- 2027 and 2028: replace all 24 Eastmere vans. Eastmere is last because its long rural routes need vans with at least 300 km of real-world range.

## Charging

Vans charge overnight at their home depot. Holt Cross has 22 charging points and, since April 2025, a 250 kWh battery that can keep about 8 vans charging during a grid outage.

## Emissions

The company reports emissions each year. Tailpipe CO2 from the fleet fell by 31% between 2019 and 2024, while parcel volume rose.

## Vehicle replacement rule

Diesel vans are not replaced with new diesel vans. A diesel van that fails or reaches 8 years old is replaced with an electric van, or, if none is available, with a short-term electric lease.
