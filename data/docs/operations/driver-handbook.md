# Driver Handbook (Summary)

## Shift limits

- Maximum driving time per shift: 9.5 hours.
- A break of at least 45 minutes is required after 4.5 hours of driving. It can be split into one 15-minute break followed by one 30-minute break.
- At least 11 hours must pass between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
- Drivers work at most 5 shifts in any 7-day period.

## Pre-shift check

Before leaving the depot, every driver completes the 12-item pre-shift check in Tally. The items include tyres, lights, mirrors, brakes, windscreen, horn, load security, fire extinguisher, first-aid kit, and, for electric vans, state of charge. An electric van must not leave with less than 80% charge unless the depot manager approves it in Tally. A failed safety item blocks the van from being assigned a route.

## Scanning

Every parcel is scanned at load, at each delivery or attempt, and at return to depot. Proof of delivery is a signature, a photo, or a safe-place photo. Drivers must not mark a parcel DELIVERED without one of these.

## Accidents and incidents

Any collision, however minor, must be reported in Tally within 1 hour and to the depot manager by phone. Injuries are reported to the depot manager at once.

## Training

New drivers complete 3 days of training, including 1 day riding along with an experienced driver. Cargo bike riders at Saltings complete an extra half-day of bike training.

## Uniform

Drivers must wear the high-visibility Brackenfield jacket while delivering. Each driver gets two jackets per year.
