# Depots

Brackenfield runs four depots. Each depot has a two-letter code used on labels and in Sorter.

## Larrow Central (LC)

- Opened: June 2016. The headquarters is next door at 14 Kiln Street.
- Vans: 38 (28 diesel, 10 electric).
- Depot manager: Tomasz Weir.
- Largest depot. Runs the central sorting hall, which handles all parcels that move between depot areas overnight.
- Sorting hall capacity: 9,000 parcels per hour.

## Eastmere (EM)

- Opened: September 2018.
- Vans: 24 (all diesel).
- Depot manager: Adaeze Okonkwo.
- Covers the most rural routes. Average route length is 142 km, compared with 61 km across the company.
- 5 of its 24 diesel vans are kept as spares in reserve; these were lent to Holt Cross during INC-2025-004.

## Holt Cross (HC)

- Opened: October 2020. First all-electric depot.
- Vans: 19 (all electric).
- Depot manager: Lena Strand.
- 22 charging points. A 250 kWh on-site battery was installed in April 2025 after INC-2025-004.

## Saltings (SA)

- Opened: July 2023.
- Vans: 12 (all electric) plus 6 electric cargo bikes.
- Depot manager: Rafael Mendes.
- The only depot with cargo bikes, used in the pedestrianised harbour district. Cargo bikes carry parcels up to 15 kg.

## Counter hours

All depot counters for collecting held parcels are open Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 19:00.
