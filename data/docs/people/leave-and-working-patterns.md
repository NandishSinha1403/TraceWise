# Leave and Working Patterns

## Annual leave

- 25 days per year plus public holidays, for full-time staff. Part-time staff get a pro-rata amount.
- After 5 years of service, annual leave rises to 28 days.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over, and must be used by 31 March of the next year.
- Drivers and depot staff cannot take leave from 20 November to 31 December (the peak period) except with the depot manager's approval.

## Parental leave

- Primary carer: 26 weeks at full pay.
- Secondary carer: 4 weeks at full pay.
- Available after 6 months of service.

## Sick leave

- Full pay for up to 10 working days in any 12 months, then statutory sick pay.
- A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 7 calendar days.

## Office working pattern

- Office staff (engineering, finance, people, support) work at least 2 days per week at the Larrow headquarters.
- Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days when teams meet in person.
- Fully remote roles must be approved by the Head of People.

## Probation

New staff have a 6-month probation period. The notice period during probation is 1 week; after probation it is 1 month.
