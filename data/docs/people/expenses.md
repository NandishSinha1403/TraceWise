# Expenses Policy

## Submitting

- Submit expense claims in Ledger within 60 days of spending the money. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
- Attach a receipt for every item over £10.
- Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Approval

- Up to £100: no approval needed beyond the automatic checks in Ledger.
- Over £100 up to £1,000: line manager approval.
- Over £1,000: Finance Director (Paul Abara) approval.

## Travel

- Rail: standard class only.
- Own car: £0.45 per mile, for business journeys only, not for commuting.
- Hotels: up to £120 per night.
- Meals while travelling overnight: up to £25 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Equipment

- Office staff working at home can claim up to £300 once for home-office equipment, in their first year.
- Laptops and phones are provided by the company and must not be bought on expenses.
