# Security Basics for All Staff

## Accounts

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Use the company password manager.
- Multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts. Hardware security keys are required for engineers with production access.
- Never share accounts, including Tally logins between drivers.

## Devices

- Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes idle.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@brackenfield.example within 1 hour.
- Driver phones are managed devices. Tally data on a lost phone is wiped remotely.

## Phishing

- Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button or forward them to security@brackenfield.example.
- Brackenfield never asks for passwords or MFA codes by email or phone.
- All staff complete phishing training once a year.

## Customer data

- Customer addresses and phone numbers are used only for delivery and support.
- Tracking photos (proof of delivery) are deleted after 90 days.
- Data requests from customers go to the support team and must be answered within 30 days.

## Production access

- Engineers get production database access only through a time-limited approval, valid for at most 8 hours.
- All production access is logged and reviewed monthly by the Platform team.
