# Service Tiers and Prices

Brackenfield offers three delivery tiers. Prices below are for business accounts, exclude VAT, and have applied since 1 January 2025.

## Standard

- Delivery within 2 business days of collection.
- Base price: £4.20 for parcels up to 2 kg.
- Booking cut-off for same-day collection: 16:00.
- Default compensation limit: £50.

## Next Day

- Delivery on the next business day.
- Base price: £6.90 for parcels up to 2 kg.
- Booking cut-off: 18:00 on the day of collection.
- Default compensation limit: £100.
- Saturday delivery is available for an extra £2.00.

## Same Day

- Delivery the same day, only when both the collection and delivery addresses are within 25 km of a single depot.
- Base price: £12.50 for parcels up to 2 kg.
- Booking cut-off: 11:00.
- Default compensation limit: £150.
- Not available on Sundays or public holidays.

## Weight pricing

- Up to 2 kg: base price only.
- Over 2 kg up to 10 kg: add £0.40 for each kilogram (or part of a kilogram) over 2 kg.
- Over 10 kg: a flat Heavy surcharge of £5.00 instead of the per-kilogram charge.
- Maximum weight: 25 kg. Heavier items are refused at collection.

Example: a 6 kg Next Day parcel costs £6.90 + 4 × £0.40 = £8.50.

Example: a 12 kg Standard parcel costs £4.20 + £5.00 = £9.20.

## Volume discounts

Accounts that send more than 2,000 parcels in a calendar month get 8% off all base prices in the following month. Surcharges are not discounted.

## Fuel and energy surcharge

There is no separate fuel surcharge. The company removed it in 2023 because more than a third of the fleet was electric.
