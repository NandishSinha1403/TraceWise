# Delivery Attempts, Safe Places, and Returns

## Attempts

Each parcel gets up to 2 delivery attempts. After a failed attempt, the driver leaves a card with a reference and the tracking status changes to ATTEMPTED. The second attempt is made on the next business day.

Same Day parcels get only 1 attempt. If it fails, the parcel moves to Next Day for the second attempt at no extra charge.

## Safe place

Recipients can nominate a safe place (for example a porch, a shed, or a neighbour) in the tracking page or by replying to the delivery email. When a parcel is left in a safe place, the driver must take a photo in Tally. Parcels with Declared Value Cover over £250 are never left in a safe place; they need a signature.

## Hold period

After the final failed attempt, the parcel goes to its depot with status HELD and is kept for 10 calendar days. The recipient can collect it with photo ID and the card reference, during depot counter hours (Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 19:00).

## Returns to sender

If a parcel is not collected within the hold period, it is returned to the sender with status RETURNING, then RETURNED.

Return fees:

- Standard: £3.00
- Next Day: £3.00
- Same Day: £5.00

Return fees are waived when the failure was caused by Brackenfield (for example, a wrong address entered by a driver).

## Refused parcels

If the recipient refuses a parcel at the door, it is returned at once without a hold period, and the normal return fee applies.
