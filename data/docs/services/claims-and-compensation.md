# Claims and Compensation

## Who can claim

Only the sender (the account holder who booked the parcel) can make a claim. Recipients must ask the sender to claim.

## Deadlines

- Lost parcel: claim within 28 days of the expected delivery date.
- Damaged parcel: claim within 7 days of the delivery date, with photos of the parcel, the packaging, and the damage.
- Late delivery: claim within 14 days of the expected delivery date. Late-delivery compensation is a refund of the delivery price only, and only for Next Day and Same Day.

A parcel is treated as lost if it has had no tracking scan for 5 business days.

## Compensation limits

Without Declared Value Cover, compensation is the lower of the item's value and the tier limit:

| Tier | Default limit |
|---|---|
| Standard | £50 |
| Next Day | £100 |
| Same Day | £150 |

## Declared Value Cover (DVC)

- Costs 1.5% of the declared value, with a minimum charge of £1.50.
- Maximum declared value: £2,000.
- Must be bought at booking; it cannot be added after collection.
- Example: declaring £400 costs £6.00 and raises the limit to £400.

## Excluded items

No compensation is paid for cash, jewellery over £500, live animals, perishable food sent by Standard, or items that were not packed to the packaging guidelines.

## How to claim

Email claims@brackenfield.example with the tracking number, the claim type, proof of value (an invoice or receipt), and photos for damage claims. The claims team replies within 3 business days and settles approved claims within 10 business days, credited to the account through Ledger.
