# Customer FAQ

**How do I track my parcel?**
Enter the tracking number on the tracking page. Business customers can use the Beacon API. Tracking updates appear within 2 minutes of a scan.

**Can I change the delivery address after booking?**
Yes, until the parcel's status is OUT_FOR_DELIVERY. Address changes to a different depot area add one business day.

**Do you deliver on Sundays?**
No. Standard and Next Day deliver Monday to Friday; Next Day can add Saturday for £2.00. Same Day runs Monday to Saturday.

**What is the biggest parcel you accept?**
25 kg, with no side longer than 120 cm, and a combined length plus girth of no more than 300 cm.

**My parcel says delivered but I don't have it.**
Check the safe-place photo on the tracking page and ask neighbours. If it is still missing after 24 hours, ask the sender to open a lost-parcel claim. Senders have 28 days from the expected delivery date.

**Can I pay on delivery?**
No. All delivery charges are paid by the sender's account.

**Do you deliver to the Saltings harbour district?**
Yes. Deliveries in the pedestrianised harbour district are made by electric cargo bike from the Saltings depot. Cargo bikes carry parcels up to 15 kg only; heavier parcels for the harbour district are delivered to the depot counter for collection.

**How do I contact support?**
Email support@brackenfield.example, Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 20:00. The target first-reply time is 4 working hours.
