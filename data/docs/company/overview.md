# Brackenfield Parcel Co. — Company Overview

> This knowledge base describes a fictional company. It was written as a test corpus for the TraceWise project. All names, places, prices, and events are invented.

Brackenfield Parcel Co. is a regional parcel delivery company headquartered in the town of Larrow. It collects parcels from businesses and individuals and delivers them across the Larrow, Eastmere, Holt Cross, and Saltings areas.

## At a glance

- Founded: 2016, by Mira Tolland (Chief Executive) and Devesh Rao (Chief Technology Officer).
- Headquarters: 14 Kiln Street, Larrow.
- Employees: about 640, of whom roughly 410 are drivers and depot staff.
- Depots: four (Larrow Central, Eastmere, Holt Cross, Saltings). See the Depots document for details.
- Fleet: 93 vans and 6 electric cargo bikes, as of the end of 2024.
- Parcels delivered in 2024: 7.8 million.
- Customer support hours: Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 20:00.

## Mission

The company's stated mission is "next-door delivery for the whole valley": fast, low-emission delivery for local businesses that cannot rely on national carriers for same-day service.

## Leadership

- Mira Tolland — Chief Executive Officer.
- Devesh Rao — Chief Technology Officer. Owns the engineering organisation and all internal systems (Tally, Sorter, Ledger, Beacon).
- Ines Corrado — Chief Operating Officer since 2021. Owns depots, drivers, and the fleet.
- Paul Abara — Finance Director. Approves expenses over £1,000.
- Grace Whitlow — Head of People.

## Service tiers

Brackenfield sells three delivery tiers: Standard, Next Day, and Same Day. Prices and cut-off times are in the Service Tiers document.
