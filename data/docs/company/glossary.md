# Glossary

**Attempt** — A delivery visit where the driver could not hand over the parcel or leave it in a safe place. Recorded with tracking status ATTEMPTED.

**Beacon** — The public parcel tracking API used by business customers. Current version is v2.

**Cut-off time** — The latest time a parcel can be booked to qualify for a service tier on that day.

**Declared Value Cover (DVC)** — Optional cover a sender buys to raise the compensation limit above the tier default. Costs 1.5% of the declared value.

**Depot code** — The two-letter code for a depot: LC (Larrow Central), EM (Eastmere), HC (Holt Cross), SA (Saltings).

**Heavy parcel** — A parcel over 10 kg. Carries a flat £5.00 surcharge. The maximum weight accepted is 25 kg.

**Hold period** — The 10 calendar days a parcel is kept at the depot after the final failed attempt, before it is returned to the sender.

**Ledger** — The internal billing and invoicing system, also used for staff expense claims.

**Pre-shift check** — The 12-item vehicle check every driver completes in Tally before leaving the depot.

**Re-plan** — An intraday Sorter run that updates routes using live traffic and new Same Day bookings. Runs every 30 minutes between 07:00 and 19:00.

**Safe place** — A location nominated by the recipient where the driver may leave a parcel if no one answers. A photo is required.

**SEV1 / SEV2 / SEV3** — Incident severity levels. See the On-call and Severity document.

**Sorter** — The internal route planning and parcel sorting system.

**Tally** — The driver mobile app used for routes, scans, proof of delivery, and pre-shift checks.
