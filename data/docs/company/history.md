# Company History and Timeline

## 2016 — Founding

Mira Tolland and Devesh Rao founded Brackenfield Parcel Co. in March 2016 after both worked at a national courier. The first depot, Larrow Central, opened in June 2016 with 11 diesel vans. The company's first customer was the Larrow Farmers' Market cooperative.

## 2017 — Next Day service

Next Day delivery launched in April 2017. Before this, only Standard (two business days) was offered.

## 2018 — Eastmere depot

The Eastmere depot opened in September 2018, doubling the delivery area. The driver app Tally replaced paper delivery sheets in the same year.

## 2019 — Same Day service

Same Day delivery launched in May 2019, at first only within 15 km of Larrow Central. The radius was extended to 25 km from any depot in 2022.

## 2020 — Holt Cross, the first all-electric depot

Holt Cross opened in October 2020 as the first depot that runs only electric vans. The company published its target of a fully electric fleet by 2028 in the same month.

## 2021 — New COO and the Sorter system

Ines Corrado joined as Chief Operating Officer in February 2021. The in-house route planning system, Sorter, went live in August 2021 and replaced a licensed third-party route planner.

## 2022 — Ledger billing system

The billing system Ledger went live in January 2022. Its database choice is recorded in ADR-003.

## 2023 — Saltings depot and cargo bikes

The Saltings depot opened in July 2023. It is the only depot that uses electric cargo bikes, for the pedestrianised harbour district.

## 2024 — Beacon API v2

Version 2 of the Beacon tracking API launched in March 2024. Sorter moved to a nightly-batch-plus-intraday-replan model (ADR-007). On 28 November 2024 the Sorter outage (INC-2024-017) delayed about 4,100 parcels.

## 2025 — Beacon v1 retired

Beacon API v1 was switched off on 31 January 2025. In February 2025 a substation fault at Holt Cross (INC-2025-004) led to the installation of on-site battery storage.
