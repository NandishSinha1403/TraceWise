# INC-2025-004: Holt Cross charging failure

- Severity: SEV2
- Date: 11 February 2025
- Duration: about 14 hours (19:30 on 10 February to 09:30 on 11 February)
- Incident commander: Lena Strand (Holt Cross depot manager), with the operations on-call

## Summary

A fault at the local electricity substation cut power to half of the Holt Cross charging points overnight. 7 of the 19 electric vans were below 80% charge in the morning and could not start their routes under the pre-shift check rule.

## Response

- Eastmere lent its 5 reserve diesel vans, which reached Holt Cross by 07:15.
- The remaining 2 routes were split across other Holt Cross vans with enough charge.
- About 300 parcels were delivered one day late.

## Root cause

The depot depended on a single grid connection with no backup. The charging management system also did not alert when charging points lost power; staff only found out at the morning pre-shift check.

## Follow-up actions

1. Install a 250 kWh on-site battery able to keep about 8 vans charging during a grid outage. Owner: Operations. Done April 2025.
2. Alert the operations on-call when more than 3 charging points lose power. Owner: Platform team. Done March 2025.
3. Keep the Eastmere reserve vans as the formal backup for Holt Cross until the battery is installed. Done.
